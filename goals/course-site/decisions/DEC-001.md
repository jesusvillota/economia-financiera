## Question and options

How do the problem sets (4 sets plus a rentas exam, with their own dedicated class sessions) appear on the site?

- **A. At the end of each chapter** as "Check yourself" exercises. Simple, but problem sessions follow a different rhythm from lectures and have more and longer exercises.
- **B. A separate "Sesiones de problemas" track** (own collection and routes): each set is a session with exercises that have seeded variants, a hint ladder, stepwise solutions and a numeric answer checker; chapters link to the relevant exercises and back.
- **C. Both**: short quizzes in chapters, full sessions in the track.

Criteria: matches how the class runs, reuse of the chapter components, cost per exercise, student value (practice with new numbers, not memorising one answer).

## Evidence

options-explained has only per-chapter quizzes (https://github.com/jesusvillota/options-explained). Source: `source/problem_sets/` (after TASK-002); counts and exercise types in `docs/AUDIT.md` section 5 (after TASK-004).

## Recommendation

**B**, with short `<Quiz>` blocks in chapters as in options-explained (so effectively C). Sessions are a first-class track with their own schema. Seeded variants and a numeric checker give new practice each visit and let tests assert every answer against `src/lib`. Recommendation only; refine after reading AUDIT section 5.
